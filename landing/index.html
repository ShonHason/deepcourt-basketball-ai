How It Works

From raw footage to
personal stats in minutes

No cameras to install. No manual input. Just upload your game and let DeepCourt handle the rest.

1

Upload your game

Upload any game video — phone camera, sideline tripod, or broadcast feed. Set the scope, period length, and overtime count.

2

Players connect themselves

Invite teammates. Each player taps the detected player in the video that matches them. Identity is locked — stats follow the person, not the jersey number.

3

Get your stats

Full box score, play-by-play narrative, and personalized learning moments — ready to share, review with your coach, or track across your season.
